## STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS BASED ON DATA

1. **Domain Authority Issue:** With 0 domain rank and only 2 months old, palaadvisory.com will struggle to rank for ANY keywords without:
   - Building backlink profile (target: 50+ quality referring domains)
   - Publishing regular content (target: 20+ indexed pages)
   - Allowing domain to age (minimum 6-12 months for trust signals)

2. **Keyword Strategy:** Avoid generic "AI operations consulting" terms (no search volume). Instead target:
   - Specific process automation keywords (AP, AR, customer service, field service)
   - Platform-specific content (NetSuite automation, QuickBooks AI, SAP intelligent automation)
   - Industry-specific use cases (healthcare automation, finance process automation)
   - Definition/educational content (workato ranks #1 for "integrators definition")

3. **Content Gap:** The market wants:
   - Specific solution content, not general consulting pages
   - Platform integration guides and definitions
   - Best practices and case studies (several KW with KD 0)
   - "How-to" and "what is" content (educational intent)

4. **Competitive Positioning:**
   - levity.ai: Small footprint but owns branded terms
   - workato.com: Dominates integration/automation education content
   - Opportunity: Target long-tail, low-KD platform-specific queries

5. **SEO Priority Actions:**
   - Build 20+ content pages targeting specific automation processes
   - Create platform integration guides (NetSuite, QuickBooks, SAP, Sage)
   - Develop glossary/definition content (definitions rank well)
   - Acquire 50+ quality backlinks (guest posts, partnerships, citations)
   - Wait 6+ months for domain authority to accumulate
