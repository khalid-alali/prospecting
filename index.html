Where I look first

Not every workflow deserves automation. These are the ones that usually do.

01

Customer Operations

Start where repeat contact reasons are stable, triage rules can be defined, and knowledge quality determines response time.

02

Finance Operations

Best for invoice intake, approval routing, duplicate detection, payout controls, and any workflow with a clear audit trail.

03

Business Operations

Useful when work crosses teams, locations, or systems and the current process depends on manual handoffs or tribal knowledge.

04

Data & Reporting

Worth fixing when leaders cannot agree on metrics, reporting takes too long, or downstream automation needs clean data to work.

How I think about ROI

Start with repetitive labor, then layer in error reduction, avoided hires, faster cycle times, and the cost of manual handoffs.

If a workflow is high volume, rule-driven, and painful to audit by hand, it is usually a candidate. If the inputs are chaotic or the owner is undefined, it usually is not.

Common questions

Why not just use ChatGPT ourselves?

The prompt is the easy part. The hard part is redesigning the workflow, connecting your systems, handling the exceptions, creating auditability, managing adoption, and measuring what changed. That is where most internal attempts stall.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Almost never. The opportunity is usually in connecting what you already have, automating the handoffs, eliminating manual steps, and making the data flow where it needs to go without a person in the middle.

What systems do you work with?

QuickBooks Enterprise, NetSuite, Sage, HubSpot, Salesforce, Airtable, Google Workspace, Slack, and most common ticketing, CRM, and ERP systems. If you have an API or a webhook, there is usually a path.

Is this strategy or implementation?

Both, but the value is in implementation. The assessment surfaces the opportunity. The engagement builds and deploys it. Strategy without a working system is just a deck.

How quickly can we see results?

Simple workflow automation can show measurable impact in weeks. More complex systems with multiple integrations or approval workflows take longer, typically two to four months to full deployment.

Find the workflows worth automating first.

Most companies are looking in the wrong places. The highest ROI is usually in the workflows that feel normal because they have been done manually for so long, no one questions them.
