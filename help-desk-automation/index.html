What gets automated

  • Routing by reason, product, and priority, into the group that actually owns it

Freshworks and Zendesk

I have done this work in both. In Freshworks that is automations, ticket fields, groups, and the knowledge base. In Zendesk that is triggers, automations, macros, views, and Guide. The design is the same: one intake, a rule for the repeat case, and an exception queue with a name on it.

The support results on the customer operations page are a platform migration of 12 million cases and a workflow change that cut contacts by 30%. Freshworks and Zendesk work starts from the queue you already have.

When a new help desk is the wrong first buy

If the team already works in Freshworks or Zendesk, the missing piece is usually the rule and the article, not a second login. Move products when routing, SLAs, or the agent workspace cannot do the job you have written down. Write that job first. The automation consultant page is the same offer for finance workflows.
