QuickBooks

QuickBooks accounts payable automation for invoices that do not match

QuickBooks Enterprise stores the vendor, the bill, and the payment. It does not read the PDF in the AP inbox, pick the GL code when a vendor shows up under three names, or chase the manager who approves anything over the threshold.

Leave the ledger in QuickBooks

The bill, the vendor record, and the payment should stay where you already close.

A second AP product becomes a problem the week someone pays a bill in QuickBooks that the other system still thinks is open. If the team closes in QuickBooks, the automation has to write the bill there.

Automate the work around the bill

  • Read the invoice from the inbox you actually use
  • Match vendor, amount, and a recurring bill or PO before anyone approves
  • Stop a duplicate before payment, including the vendor spelled three ways
  • Route the approval with a written threshold, and a backup if that person is out
  • Flag a payout anomaly before money leaves

When to buy a tool instead

Buy an AP product when you need a vendor portal and outbound payments, and the volume is past what this team can touch. Wire QuickBooks when the formats repeat and the delay is getting a clean bill into the company file.

If most invoices are exceptions, write the exception rule before you buy either. The best practices are the same on every ledger.
