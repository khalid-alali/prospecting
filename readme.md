## FAQ

### Why not just use ChatGPT ourselves?

Because the hard part is not prompting ChatGPT. The hard part is redesigning the workflow, connecting systems, handling exceptions, creating auditability, managing adoption, and measuring ROI.

### Do we need to replace our existing software?

Usually no. Most companies already have enough tools. The opportunity is usually connecting them better, automating repetitive work, and eliminating manual handoffs.

### Is this strategy or implementation?

Both, but implementation-focused. The assessment identifies the opportunity. The real value comes from building and deploying workflows that reduce cost.

### How quickly can we see results?

Simple workflow automation opportunities can often show value in weeks. More complex operational systems may take longer depending on integrations, approvals, and process complexity.
